Required proof interface for a revised BendTT kernel
2026-10-16

Scope

Preserve the revised checker definitions and all advertised safety guarantees.
Replace old typing claims with their revised counterparts. Do not keep a second
checker to obtain safety from the old checker. Retain original runtime reduction
and the type-independent termination proof.

Original public claims

Claim.sound:
  Revised.book_check. Accepted books have Revised.WellTyped and Book.Live.

Claim.confluent:
  Original confluent. Original Pars has a common reduct for each reduction pair.
  Also retain GlobalDiamond.confluent for canonical erased comparison reduction.

Claim.sr:
  Revised.sr. Original Par preserves Revised.Typed in all contexts.
  Keep Revised.pars_sr and Revised.eval_preservation as direct consequences.

Claim.progress:
  Revised.progress. Empty-context typed terms are values or take original Eval
  steps, under Revised.WellTyped and Book.Live.

Claim.halts:
  Original halts. Book.Live, Closed, and Term.Live imply termination under Eval.
  This claim needs no typing premise and no revised proof.

Claim.empty:
  Revised.empty_value. A value cannot have the empty enumeration type.

Claim.consistent:
  Revised.checked_no_empty. A definition found in an accepted book cannot have
  a declared type joined to Empty. This rules out old conversion to Empty too.

Additional advertised guarantees

ExecutableOrder.join_refl, join_symm, join_trans, join_sub, join_old:
  Revised equality is an equivalence relation, is stable under raw substitution,
  and includes old conversion.

EquationBridge.diagnostic_join and diagnostic_substitution_join:
  Successful finite comparison implies revised equality. That equality survives
  raw substitution. This is not comparator completeness after substitution.

ExecutableOrder.diagnostic_qge_sound and diagnostic_fits_sound:
  Executable quantity order and type fitting use the same revised equality.
  NewFits uses that equality on both sides of its semantic quantity implication.

Revised.typed_sub and typed_inst:
  Revised typing is stable under well-typed substitution and instantiation.

FullDataSafety.typed_eta_factorization:
  A comparison reduction from an erased typed term factors into original
  reduction of that term followed by eta reduction of the erased result.

FullDataSafety.typed_kind_exposure and typed_data_exposure:
  A formed type equal to a rigid type constructor exposes that constructor by
  original reduction. These statements have no open simulation premise.

Revised.canon_data:
  A value of a type formed at Data satisfies the original Data predicate.

TypedValueDataEquality.data_iff_same_type_join:
  Equal values with the same revised type have the same Data classification.

Revised.trace_preservation, checked_definition_trace_preservation, and
data_kind_trace_preservation:
  Original runtime traces preserve the exact revised type. A checked definition
  keeps its declared type. Runtime evaluation preserves a Data-kind judgment.

Revised.data_results, no_closed_empty, checked_definition_data, and
checked_data_results:
  Closed live terms at formed Data types reach Data values with the same type.
  Closed live terms cannot inhabit Empty. Checked-book forms discharge book
  formation and liveness premises where their statements specify this.

Candidate.accepted_text_no_empty, if the experiment's text wrapper is retained:
  Accepted text parses to a book accepted by the proved checker. No definition
  in that book has a declared type joined to Empty. This theorem does not prove
  parsing correctness against a separate text specification.

Safe removal candidates

Remove old checker soundness, old typed preservation, old progress, and old
typed consistency proof paths after their revised counterparts replace them.
Remove imports and declarations used only for rejected designs, counterexamples,
or empirical tests. Keep those files in the research evidence package.
Remove duplicate executable definitions after exact correspondence checks.
Retain ordinary reduction algebra used by the revised proof and termination.

Required mathematical groups

1. Erasure, substitution, erased reduction, and confluence.
2. Comparator soundness and coherent semantic quantity fitting.
3. Revised typing substitution, inversion, and original reduction preservation.
4. Hereditary head safety, eta-path simulation, and typed factorization.
5. Data canonical forms, progress, and consistency with original termination.

The source import closure is not a minimum proof size. Declaration pruning must
follow actual Lean constant dependencies. The result must compile with source
proofs and an explicit public interface. Do not hide proof terms in generated
binaries or move proofs outside the allowed file to satisfy its size cap.

Limits

The claims do not prove safe.ts correctness, comparator completeness, an adequate
fixed fuel bound, source-checker soundness, text-parser correctness, native code
generation correctness, or runtime performance. Exact quantity-sensitive typing
is not preserved by arbitrary eta contraction. Keep eta in comparison reduction.
Erasure retains All, Sig, Tup, and Let quantity tags. Ordinary let reduction
can still remove a Let tag.
